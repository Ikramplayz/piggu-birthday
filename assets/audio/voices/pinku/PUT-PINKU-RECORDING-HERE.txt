PUT PINKU'S VOICE CLIPS IN THIS FOLDER.

You said you have a screen recording with Pinku's real voice. A screen
recording is a video file, and browsers cannot use the video's audio, so
it has to be turned into sound files first. The easiest free way:

   1. Go to any free "video to mp3" converter on the web, OR use
      VLC Media Player (free) -> Media -> Convert / Save.
   2. Load your screen recording.
   3. Convert it to MP3.
   4. You now have ONE long mp3 of the whole recording.

Then cut that long file into four short clips. In VLC, or any free audio
editor (Audacity is free and good), trim each line and export it.

Save the four clips in THIS folder with EXACTLY these names.

mp3 is best, but ogg and wav work too - keep the same name before the
dot (so pinku_dialogue_01.wav is fine). You do NOT have to convert to
mp3 if your clips are already ogg or wav.

    pinku_dialogue_01.mp3   <- when the bosses appear:
                                "Oh! Piggu, you actually came. Happy birthday, little piggy!"

    pinku_dialogue_02.mp3   <- the first time you hit a boss:
                                "Careful! I am not going easy on you today!"

    pinku_dialogue_03.mp3   <- when Pinku is defeated:
                                "Okay, okay! You win. Paddy, you are on your own!"

    pinku_dialogue_04.mp3   <- during the birthday ending:
                                "Happy birthday, Piggu. We love you."

If your recording says something different, that is completely fine -
just keep the four filenames and the game will play your real audio.

IMPORTANT: only use these recordings if you have Pinku's permission.
If you would rather not, do nothing: the same lines appear as on-screen
text and the game works normally without any voice.

You can delete this text file once your clips are in.
