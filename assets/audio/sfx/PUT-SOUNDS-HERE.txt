Put the sound effect files in THIS folder.

The game looks for exactly these filenames:

    jump.mp3             soft cute jump
    land.mp3             gentle landing
    collect_item.mp3     everyday pickup
    collect_present.mp3  present opened
    star_chime.mp3       star collected
    checkpoint.mp3       signpost reached
    hurt.mp3             soft cartoon damage
    victory.mp3          level finished
    boss_appear.mp3      bosses arrive
    boss_attack.mp3      a boss throws something
    boss_defeat.mp3      a boss is beaten
    celebration.mp3      birthday ending cheer
    ui_click.mp3         button tap
    ui_pause.mp3         pause and resume
    finish_bell.mp3      birthday bell at the finish line
    balloon.mp3          balloon squeak when you pass one
    cake_sparkle.mp3     cake twinkle when you pass one

Keep those exact names. You can delete this text file once your sounds are in.

Any file that is missing is simply silent - the game carries on normally.
